SumGrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · App: SumGrid (Android package com.sumgrid.app, iOS bundle com.sumgrid.app)

SumGrid is a puzzle game (Sudoku, Number Sums, Sum Sudoku and Kakuro) made by SumGrid Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. We keep it short because the app collects very little.

1. Information that stays on your device

Your game progress, levels completed, statistics, streaks, saved games, settings and the optional name you type in the Account screen are stored only on your device. We do not have servers that receive this data and we cannot see it. If you use “Copy backup”, the app creates a backup code on your device; it is only shared if you choose to paste or send it somewhere yourself. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

2. You don’t need an account

SumGrid works without signing in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of SumGrid shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, if you allow it) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language);
  • interactions with ads (views and taps) and diagnostic / performance data.

This information is collected and used by Google as an independent controller under Google’s own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool; you can change your choice at any time in Account → About & privacy → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iOS you can decline tracking when asked, or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

If you buy SumGrid Pro (when available), ads are turned off and the ads SDK no longer requests ads.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, SumGrid schedules a daily reminder on your device. No information is sent to us to do this. You can turn reminders off in the app (Account → Daily reminder) or in your phone’s settings.

5. Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

6. Children

SumGrid is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

7. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone, because we do not collect it. The only third party involved is Google AdMob, as described in section 3.

8. Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by your device’s own security. Information collected by Google is handled under Google’s policies and retention periods. Communication with ad servers is encrypted in transit.

9. Your rights

Because your game data never leaves your device, you control it directly: you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. For questions about data processed by Google, see the links in section 3. You can also contact us with any privacy request.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
